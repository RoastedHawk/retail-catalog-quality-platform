# Catalog Quality Platform Experiment Design

## Objective

Determine whether catalog quality recommendations help merchants improve product feed quality.

---

## Hypothesis

Providing prioritized recommendations will reduce catalog quality issues and improve overall catalog health.

---

## Experiment Setup

### Control Group

Merchants receive:

- Catalog quality score
- Issue list

### Treatment Group

Merchants receive:

- Catalog quality score
- Issue list
- Prioritized recommendations
- Business impact explanations

---

## Success Metrics

### Primary Metric

Average catalog quality score improvement.

### Secondary Metrics

- Reduction in products with critical issues
- Reduction in missing images
- Reduction in missing categories
- Reduction in invalid pricing

### Operational Metrics

- Recommendation adoption rate
- Time to issue resolution

---

## Example Evaluation Window

30 days

---

## Risks

### Recommendation Fatigue

Too many recommendations may reduce merchant engagement.

### False Positives

Some recommendations may not be relevant for every product category.

### Limited Context

Rules-based recommendations may not reflect merchant-specific goals.

---

## Future Iterations

Potential future improvements:

- Category-specific recommendations
- Performance-informed prioritization
- Historical outcome integration
- ML-assisted recommendation ranking
